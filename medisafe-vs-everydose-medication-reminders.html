Medisafe vs EveryDose
for medication reminders

Medisafe and EveryDose both cover reminders, dose records, medication information, sharing, and paid upgrades. Medisafe gives more emphasis to complex schedules and Medfriend alerts, while EveryDose centres medication leaflets, progress emails, watch actions, and a simpler daily schedule.

Published August 21, 2026 · Last reviewed August 21, 2026

A note from Pillr

This article is published by Pillr. We make a medication tracking app, but this page compares Medisafe and EveryDose for people deciding between those two options.

Quick answer

Choose Medisafe if you want complex or as-needed schedules, refill reminders, detailed medication reports, health measurements, and Medfriend alerts for a chosen family member or caregiver.

Choose EveryDose if you want medication reminders beside leaflets, interaction alerts, progress emails, smartwatch actions, current iPhone support for every-X-hours schedules, and an optional paid family and health journal.

Both are available on iPhone and Android and both can be downloaded free. Paid access, medication limits, and feature boundaries can vary by country, store, and plan, so check the current terms in your region.

Who this comparison is for

This comparison is for someone choosing between two medication-management apps, especially when complex schedules, as-needed medication, caregiver visibility, medication information, dose history, refills, reports, or watch access will decide the fit.

It is not a guide to choosing medication, changing a dose, interpreting an interaction alert, or deciding what to do after a missed dose.

Key differences

Medisafe is care-partner and schedule led. Its current Google Play page describes complex schedules, as-needed medication, Medfriend alerts, refill reminders, medication reports, health measurements, and an interaction checker.

EveryDose is medication-information and progress-sharing led. Its current feature page combines daily reminders with taken, skipped, or snoozed actions, medication leaflets, interaction alerts, history, progress emails, and watch actions.

The overlap is substantial. Both support medication reminders, dose history, interaction information, refill tools, health records, sharing, and paid upgrades. The useful difference is how another person stays informed and which tools sit in the standard daily experience.

Detailed comparison by use case

If schedules and dose actions are the main job

Medisafe gives broad public emphasis to complex schedules, as-needed medication, custom reminders, time-zone support, and medication reporting. It may fit better when several medications follow different patterns.

EveryDose supports daily, as-needed, specific-day, weekly, monthly, and cycle schedules. Its current iPhone release also lists every-X-hours schedules, a dedicated as-needed history, and improved reports and exports.

Before choosing, check whether a late entry keeps the actual time, how snoozes affect later reminders, and whether history clearly separates taken, skipped, and unrecorded doses.

If another person needs visibility

Medisafe's Medfriend workflow is the clearer fit when a chosen family member or caregiver should receive an alert after a dose is not recorded. Its wider family tools can also support shared medication management, subject to the current permissions and paid limits.

EveryDose includes scheduled or one-off progress emails for invited friends. EveryDose Plus also lists up to five additional profiles or linked loved ones. That may fit a household record, but it is not the same as a real-time missed-dose alert.

If medication information matters

Both apps list drug-interaction information. Medisafe places an interaction checker beside its broader medication manager. EveryDose also lists medication leaflets, drug images, food information, and interaction alerts inside its standard feature set.

Neither app should be treated as the final word on interactions, side effects, missed doses, or timing. Confirm medication questions with a pharmacist or prescribing clinician.

If refills and reports matter

Medisafe lists refill reminders plus daily, weekly, and monthly medication reporting with a shareable logbook. Check which medication, report, refill, and sharing tools are included in the current plan where you live.

EveryDose lists taken and skipped history, weekly progress emails, medication-list sharing, and improved iPhone reports and exports. Its paid Plus plan adds pill counts and refill reminders.

Open a sample report before relying on either app. Check whether it shows actual intake times, skipped entries, corrections, notes, quantities, and the date range you need.

If privacy and account setup matter

Medisafe says basic use does not require registration, while connected sharing, caregiver, subscription, and service features use Medisafe systems. Its current privacy policy explains how personal and health information can be processed for reminders, sharing, analytics, support, and optional programmes.

EveryDose's current privacy policy describes account information and explains that deleting the app does not by itself delete an account or personal information. Its security page describes cloud hosting, encryption, authentication, monitoring, and security reviews.

Review current sign-in, deletion, export, analytics, backup, caregiver, and sharing rules before entering a full medication history.

Where Medisafe fits better

  • You want Medfriend alerts for a chosen family member or caregiver.
  • You need complex or as-needed schedule support.
  • You want refill reminders and broad medication reports in the same general manager.
  • You want Apple Watch support listed alongside iPhone and Android access.

Where EveryDose fits better

  • You want medication leaflets, interaction alerts, and food information near the daily schedule.
  • You want scheduled progress emails for a trusted person.
  • You want smartwatch alerts and one-tap taken actions on supported watches.
  • You use iPhone and want every-X-hours reminders or a dedicated as-needed history.

When neither app may be enough

You may need a different tool if your priority is a local-only medication record, a specific pharmacy or clinician portal, a specialist export, or a family role that neither app provides clearly.

If neither app fits the way you track ADHD medication, Pillr is built around reminders, dose history, estimated focus windows, reflections, refill planning, and medication notes. It is a separate option, not part of the Medisafe and EveryDose comparison above.

What to verify before choosing

  • Whether the app supports your exact prescribed schedule and as-needed workflow.
  • How taken, skipped, late, snoozed, corrected, and unrecorded doses appear in history.
  • Which medication, refill, report, family, profile, and sharing tools need a paid plan.
  • Whether you need a real-time caregiver alert, a progress email, or a separate profile.
  • Whether interaction information and medication leaflets are available in your country.
  • How sign-in, deletion, analytics, backup, syncing, sharing, and health-data handling work.

Is Medisafe or EveryDose better for medication reminders?

Medisafe may fit better if complex schedules, Medfriend alerts, family management, and broad medication reporting matter most. EveryDose may fit better if medication leaflets, interaction alerts, progress emails, watch actions, and a medication-first daily schedule matter more.

Do Medisafe and EveryDose support as-needed medication?

Yes. Medisafe lists as-needed medication and complex schedule support. EveryDose lists as-needed schedules and its current iPhone release includes a dedicated as-needed history. Check how either app records quantities, timing, corrections, and anything left unrecorded.

Can Medisafe and EveryDose share medication progress with someone else?

Yes, but the workflows differ. Medisafe's Medfriend feature can alert a chosen person when a dose is not recorded. EveryDose offers progress emails and lists multiple profiles in its paid Plus plan. Check the current permissions and plan limits before choosing.

Do Medisafe or EveryDose replace medical advice?

No. They are reminder and record-keeping tools. Questions about doses, timing, interactions, side effects, and missed medication need advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
