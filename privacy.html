Privacy

A clear view of
how Pillr handles data

Pillr is designed to keep medication tracking private and simple. This page explains what stays on your device, what is optional, and what to expect before you use the app.

Updated June 28, 2026

Short version Your medication data stays on your device. Pillr keeps checked recovery copies on your iPhone, and you choose whether to restore one. Appointment audio, transcripts, and notes also stay on your iPhone. Apple Health access and other AI tools are optional.

What stays on your device by default

Your medication entries, logs, notes, appointment records, and routine data are stored on your device by default. You do not need to create an account to start using Pillr.

Appointment recordings and transcripts

If you use My Appointment, appointment audio, transcripts, and appointment notes stay on your iPhone. Pillr does not send appointment recordings, transcripts, or appointment text to cloud AI tools, analytics services, or a Pillr account.

On-device recovery copies

Pillr keeps a current checked recovery copy and, when available, an earlier checked copy on your iPhone. In Settings, you can review and choose either copy before restoring it. Pillr does not offer cloud backup, cloud restore, or syncing.

Optional Apple Health access

Pillr may offer optional Apple Health features. If you turn those on, Apple Health permissions are controlled by you inside iPhone settings.

Other optional AI features

Some other AI tools are optional. Because those tools may rely on internet access, they should only be used if you want them. They are not required for basic medication reminders, tracking, or My Appointment records.

No medical advice

Pillr helps you remember medication and keep records. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Questions or support

If you have a privacy question, use the contact and support form.

At a glance

  • No account required
  • On-device by default
  • Appointment audio stays on iPhone
  • Transcripts and notes stay on iPhone
  • Checked recovery copies on your iPhone
  • Optional Apple Health access
  • Other optional AI tools
